A close look

The small line above your name

Your logo was drawn with the name large and the line above it small. That is the right order and I am keeping it exactly that way. The name is roughly eight times taller than the small line, and that gap is doing real work.

The two are built for two different distances. Haru is for the street. People read it from a car, from the crosswalk, from the far sidewalk. The small line is for your door. It reads when someone is standing at your window deciding whether to come in, and that is the exact moment it matters most.

So you should expect this: from across Cary you will see the name, the triangle, and the green. Up close you get the rest. That is how the logo was designed to work, and painting it any other way would mean changing your logo.
