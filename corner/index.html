Why the words do not line up

In this style of letters, every letter takes up the same amount of room. A wide letter and a thin letter get the same space. That is what makes the sign look neat and even.

So the length of a line only depends on how many letters are in the word.

What I meant by flat

Flat means the edge makes one straight line, up and down. Right now the left side is flat. Every line starts in the same place.

Ragged is the opposite word. It means each line stops in a different place. Right now the right side is ragged.

A sign can be flat on one side, flat on both sides, or flat on neither. None of them is wrong. They just look different.
